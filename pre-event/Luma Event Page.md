About Event

# ​ **New York is where finance gets built. Now it’s time to show Wall Street what AI can do.**

​Build a real AI product for finance in one day, solve problems coming from companies working in the space, and put what you build in front of founders, operators, hiring teams and technology companies.

​🔷 **Build with PRISM by Block Convey: Build → Observe → Improve → Prove → Demo.**

## ​🚀 What You Leave With

​🏆 **Up to $5,000 in cash prizes + partner credits**, made possible by our sponsors.

​🥇 **1st Place:** **$1,000 cash + $1,000+ worth of sponsor credits and technology access.** Walk away with cash, tools to keep building after the hackathon, and direct visibility with our partner companies.

​🥈 **Two Runner-Up Awards:** **$500 cash + $500+ in sponsor credits each.**

​💼 **Top-performing builders will also have the opportunity to speak directly with the recruiting teams at Maximor and TechNovaTime**, turning a one-day project into a potential interview conversation. **Live Interview**

​This is not just about winning a hackathon.

​ **Build something useful. Prove it works. Get it in front of people who can open the next door.**

# ​🧑‍💻 Technology Partners

​Our technology partners are giving teams tools and credits that can help them move faster during the hackathon.

​ **GIDE**

​A local-first AI coding environment where builders can run models locally, work offline, bring their own cloud models and keep control over what an AI coding agent changes.

​ **[generativeide.com](https://generativeide.com/?utm_source=luma)**

​ **Prelint**

​AI can generate code quickly. Prelint helps answer the next question: **did the AI actually build what the product was supposed to do?**

​It reviews AI-written changes against product requirements, rules and decisions.

​ **[prelint.com](https://prelint.com/?utm_source=luma)**
